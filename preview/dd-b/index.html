Dice Drop

A drop and merge game

Drop and rotate dice pieces. Match adjacent dice of the same value and they merge up. Combine 6s to clear them from the board, chain one merge into the next, and climb the global leaderboard.

How it plays.

Three rules, and the board fills up faster than you expect.

01

Drop and rotate

Pieces fall onto a five wide board. Rotate them on the way down.

02

Match and merge

Two dice of the same value sitting next to each other merge into the next number up.

03

Clear the sixes

Combine 6s and they leave the board. One merge can trigger the next.
