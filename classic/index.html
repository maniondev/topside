How it plays.

Roll a cube across a grid and match each face to its target tile. Plan your path, think ahead, and find the perfect route.

01

Roll the cube

Every move tips the cube onto a new face. The face you land on is the one that counts.

02

Match the tile

Each target tile wants a particular face. Arrive with the wrong one and you have to go around again.

03

Plan the route

The order you solve the tiles in decides whether the last one is reachable at all.
